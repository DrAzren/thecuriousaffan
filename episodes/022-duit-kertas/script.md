## Facts used

- Most banknotes are printed on cotton-based paper, which is stronger than
  wood-pulp paper.
- In Malaysia's current series, RM1 and RM5 are polymer (plastic) notes.
- Security features: watermark (hidden picture seen against light), security
  thread, raised (intaglio) ink, unique serial numbers.
- Bank Negara Malaysia issues the currency and distributes it to banks.

The notes drawn in the cover are made-up cartoon notes, not copies of real
ringgit designs.
