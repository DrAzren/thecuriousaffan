## Fact check

- Gold is about 19 times denser than water and about 7 times denser than
  quartz sand, so in a pan it sinks while the sand is washed over the rim.
- Typical hard-rock ore holds roughly 1–5 g of gold per tonne. A car is about
  1–1.5 t, and 1 g of gold is about 0.05 cm³, roughly the size of a mung bean.
- About 210,000 t of gold has been mined in all of history (World Gold
  Council). At 19.3 t/m³ that is about 11,000 m³, roughly four to five Olympic
  pools (2,500 m³ each).
- Gold does not rust or tarnish. Gold objects thousands of years old are still
  shiny.
- Malaysia has a long history of gold panning and mining, for example around
  Raub and Kuala Lipis in Pahang.
